The 60-second read

At Universe: Fusion, connected cloud sessions, app projects and richer automation shifted the focus toward coordinated agent work. Their release phases are not uniform.

Since the event: Air and native Xcode integrations expand where you work; usage reporting and review APIs strengthen visibility and control.

Review before enabling: Claude Fable 5.2 is GA but has default provider retention and an off-by-default admin policy. The exemption is eligibility-based and time-bound, not a universal ZDR guarantee.

Details and primary citations are attached to every update below.

03 / A few important distinctions

Availability is more than a badge.

GA
The cited release explicitly says generally available. It can still roll out gradually or require enablement.
Public preview
Available for evaluation subject to the stated plan, platform and policy prerequisites. Not a GA commitment.
Experimental
An opt-in experiment with narrower support. Do not equate Fusion's local-routing preview with Fusion GA.
Released / other
The source announces availability, rollout or a transition without establishing a uniform GA/preview phase. The guide does not invent one.
Mixed
A rollup contains capabilities with different release phases. Read the limitations rather than applying one label to everything.
Eligibility
Plans describe the cited feature, not every surface of the wider product. Unspecified plan details and governance parity are marked as gaps, not inferred.

The window

Universe 2026 ran Oct 28-29 in San Francisco, per the Official Universe event page ↗. The baseline uses Oct 28-29 publication dates; strictly after the event means Oct 30-Nov 10. Nov 10 is incomplete at the research cutoff. Publication dates use the source's calendar date; API timestamps did not supply a timezone.

The evidence

What is not claimed

No private roadmap, exhaustive client patch history, tenant-level rollout validation, contractual pricing or GHES parity. Current docs clarify present behavior; they do not prove that every clarification first shipped during this window. No directly relevant post was identified on Oct 30-Nov 2, Oct 29 or Nov 7-8 in the reviewed changelog, not proof of no product changes.
